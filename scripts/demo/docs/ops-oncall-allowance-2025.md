# SOP-OPS-07: On-Call Duty Allowance & P1 Incident Response Policy (2025)

- **Document Code:** `SOP-OPS-07`
- **Revision:** `1.0`
- **Effective Date:** `2025-01-01`
- **Owner Department:** Engineering (`Kỹ thuật`)
- **Audience:** Engineering team members only (restricted)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the on-call duty allowance rates, shift limits, and P1 incident response SLA for Engineering team members during 2025.

## 2. On-Call Allowance Rates

1. **Weekday on-call shift (Monday–Friday, 18:00–08:30 ICT):** **200,000 VND/night**.
2. **Weekend or public holiday on-call shift (24-hour coverage):** **400,000 VND/day**.
3. Allowances are paid with monthly payroll based on the approved PagerDuty schedule.

## 3. Shift Limits & P1 Response SLA

1. An engineer may be scheduled for at most **8 on-call shifts per month**.
2. For **Priority 1 (P1)** production incidents, the primary on-call engineer must acknowledge and begin triage within **30 minutes**.
3. Unacknowledged P1 alerts escalate automatically to the Engineering Lead after **30 minutes**.
