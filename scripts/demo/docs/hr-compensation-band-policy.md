# SOP-HR-04: Annual Compensation Review & Performance Bonus Policy

- **Document Code:** `SOP-HR-04`
- **Revision:** `1.4`
- **Effective Date:** `2026-09-15`
- **Owner Department:** Human Resources (`Nhân sự`)
- **Audience:** HR department only (restricted — confidential)

## 1. Scope

This document governs annual salary band adjustments and performance bonus calculations for full-time employees. It is restricted to the Human Resources (`Nhân sự`) department.

## 2. Review Schedule

- Performance calibration closes on **December 15** each year.
- Compensation committee sign-off is completed by **January 10**.
- Approved salary adjustments take effect on **February 1** payroll.

## 3. Performance Bonus Multipliers

| Performance Rating | Base Bonus Target | Eligibility Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Exceeds Expectations (`E`) | **2.5 months** base salary | ≥ 6 months tenure before Dec 31 |
| Meets Expectations (`M`) | **1.2 months** base salary | ≥ 6 months tenure before Dec 31 |
| Developing (`D`) | **0.5 months** base salary | Manager discretion |
| Needs Improvement (`NI`) | **0.0 months** | Ineligible |

## 4. Exception Approval

Any out-of-cycle salary adjustment exceeding **12%** requires written co-approval from both the **Head of HR** and the **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)**.
