# SOP-OPS-07: On-Call Duty Allowance & P1 Incident Response Policy (2026)

- **Document Code:** `SOP-OPS-07`
- **Revision:** `2.0`
- **Effective Date:** `2026-11-01`
- **Owner Department:** Engineering (`Kỹ thuật`)
- **Audience:** Engineering team members only (restricted)

## 1. Purpose

This updated policy supersedes Revision 1.0 (2025) and defines the revised on-call duty allowance rates, shift limits, and P1 incident response SLA for Engineering team members effective November 2026.

## 2. On-Call Allowance Rates

1. **Weekday on-call shift (Monday–Friday, 18:00–08:30 ICT):** **350,000 VND/night**.
2. **Weekend or public holiday on-call shift (24-hour coverage):** **700,000 VND/day**.
3. Allowances are paid with monthly payroll based on the approved PagerDuty schedule.

## 3. Shift Limits & P1 Response SLA

1. An engineer may be scheduled for at most **6 on-call shifts per month**.
2. For **Priority 1 (P1)** production incidents, the primary on-call engineer must acknowledge and begin triage within **10 minutes**.
3. Unacknowledged P1 alerts escalate automatically to the Engineering Lead after **10 minutes**.
