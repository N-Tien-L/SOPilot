## Findings and limitations

**F1: reasoning leaks into the answer (P0, blocks UC-04 and FR-11). Cause confirmed, fixed in V2-42.** The provider split the stream correctly: the raw Modal stream holds `…cw\n</think>\n\n<answer>`, `generate.thinking` is `True` in the container, and `ThinkSplitter` (`providers/llm.py`) yielded the reasoning as `Thought`. `_pump` (`services/ai-service/src/sopilot_ai/conversations/steps.py:136`, V2-10) handed every delta on as `str(value)`, which turns a `Thought` (a `str` subclass) into a plain `str`, so the `isinstance(delta, Thought)` check in `GenerateStep` (`steps.py:436`, V2-32) was never true and all reasoning went down the answer path; the stray "cw" is the model's own text before `</think>`. Minimal reproduction: the splitter's output `[('Thought', 'plan it.cw\n'), ('str', 'Answer [1].')]` became `[('str', …), ('str', …)]` after `_pump`. Fix: `yield value` (`V2-42`, ticket `V2-42-ai-service-reasoning-leaks-into-answer.md` on branch `fix/ai-reasoning-pump`). The output guard did not catch the leak because it trips only on the canary or a 40-character verbatim run of the rules, and the reasoning paraphrased them (FR-13, decision below).

**F2: why the suites stayed green.** V2-32 tested the splitter (`test_llm_provider.py:413-457`) and `_ThoughtStream` (`test_thought_stream.py`) separately; no test ran a `Thought` through `_pump` and `GenerateStep`. V2-42 adds `test_pump_keeps_reasoning_marked_as_reasoning` (unit) and `test_the_models_reasoning_goes_out_as_reasoning_never_as_answer_text` (integration); both failed on the unchanged code.

**F3: FR-20 mismatch (resolved in the doc).** `docs/architecture/components/core-api.md:501` said "`LOGIN` is recorded in the workspace chosen at sign-in"; the code records it in the personal workspace only (`IdentityService.java:114-126`), so a team workspace's log shows no sign-ins of its members. Owner decision 2026-11-16: keep the code, correct the doc (done on this branch). `LOGIN` still has no test.

**F4: environment findings.** The Docker VM at 5 GB OOM-killed the worker (raised to 10 GB in `.wslconfig`); `ai-worker` has no restart policy in compose; a stalled Hugging Face Hub download hung the first question (pings only, nothing logged) until the container was restarted; the owner's `SOPILOT_RERANK_BASE_URL` used the `sopilot-vllm-serve` label (404) instead of `sopilot-vllm-rerank` (200), and a rerank error (404, or a 30 s timeout while the reranker starts) fails the whole question with `INTERNAL_ERROR`. Compose hard-codes `container_name` and `networks.sopilot-v2.name`, so `-p sopilot-parity` alone collides with the owner's stack.

**F5: known weak spots, not failures.** 15 guard cases (including `pa-01..pa-08`) pass the input rules as recorded gaps; a typo greeting, abuse-only text and out-of-scope questions end as `NO_EVIDENCE` rather than a template (`guard-tests/results.md`, cases 02a, 04a, 06a/b, `gap`).
